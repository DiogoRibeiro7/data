## Interpretation

- Count participants once per study, not per effect. Lien has two strata with unknown stratum n.
- The cohort OR is the review's conversion of an HR using an approximately 2% baseline risk. Do not convert it again. Primary-source effect metrics and conversions are not independently audited.
- Exposure definitions include soft drinks, heterogeneous frequency/volume thresholds and a continuous contrast. Do not interpret the pooled result as a uniform intervention or causal effect.
- Adjustment codes refer to Table 1's footnote; they are not harmonized covariate names. The sex/gender codes are retained separately. Quality scales have different maxima by design.
- Yu's study size is 173 cases plus 159 controls; separate mean ages are in the summary JSON. No age or sex-specific counts have been imputed.
- Article narrative states an upper age of 16, while Table 1 reports two studies spanning 6–18 years; the table's ranges are retained. The source remains authoritative for inconsistencies.
- Pooled statistics are published benchmarks. An independent reproduction requires log-OR standard errors, within-study combination, Paule-Mandel and Hartung-Knapp; rounding can cause discrepancies.
- No restricted source PDFs or individual participant data are stored.
